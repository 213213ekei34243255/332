How it works

  1. Quick camera and microphone check
  2. A short face verification
  3. A spoken conversation with an AI interviewer (15 minutes)

Before you start

  • Find a quiet, well-lit room and sit alone
  • Use a laptop or desktop; headphones help
  • Just talk naturally — the interviewer replies when you pause
  • Keep this tab open until the end

Interview language

Choose the language you are most comfortable speaking. The interviewer will speak and listen in it, and you can mix in English words as you normally would.

Recording notice. The whole session (video and audio) is recorded and your answers are assessed with the help of AI. The hiring team reviews every recording and assessment; people make the final decision.
